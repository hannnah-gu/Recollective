About Recollective

Recollective is a website designed to improve the day-to-day lives of dementia patients.

With the implementation of AI camera recognition and location tracking, Recollective aims to direct the "Perfect Day" for individuals with dementia.

Start by having the caretaker enter general information regarding scheduling specifications and location landmarks.
