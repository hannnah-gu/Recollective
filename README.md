# Recollective

## ABOUT

Recollective is a web application aimed at making daily tasks more accessible to people with dementia while also providing caregivers with tools to assist their residents.

The goal of Recollective is to help residents maintain independence by providing simple visual and voice based guidance throughout the day to hopefully provide them the "Perfect Day".

Some of the biggest challenges faced by people with dementia center around decrease in pattern recognition. Pattern recognition, maintaining routines, and understanding the context of their location can become difficult when individuals with dementia are without a caregiver.

Recollective aims to provide an additional layer of support between the resident and their caregiver.

## Features

1) The simple design and bright, contrasting colors of Recollective prevent users with dementia from becoming overwhelmed or confused.

2) Caregivers may customize Recollective for their residents by identifying key locations within the home. Furthermore, caregivers may customize a schedule for their residents that the AI will provide reminders for when approaching the set time.

3) Using location services, key locations are identified to guide individuals with dementia toward areas of their home. Key locations include:
- bedroom
- bathroom
- kitchen
- closet
- main door

4) Object recognition with AI helps residents identify certain objects in their home.

5) Dementia may cause an individual to have difficulty reading. AI text-to-speech allows residents in later stages of dementia to better understand and interact with the website.

6) The help button contacts the caregiver through SMS. Its bright red appearance helps guide residents toward it in moments of need.


## Website Structure

Recollective works through a 2-step process. The caregiver begins by entering their contact information, the resident's schedule, and identifying important locations within the house.

The device is then passed on to the resident, who is able to receive audio and text reminders to guide them through their daily activities. The website allows them to contact their caregiver for help through SMS or receive support through the website's AI.


## Tech Stack

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Browser APIs: Camera, Geolocation, Web Speech, Local Storage

Object recognition: TensorFlow.js, COCO-SSD object detection model

How we coded: VS Code, Live Share, GitHub


## Future Plans for Recollective + Limitations

- Currently, GPS accuracy is limited within the home due to the close proximity of rooms. For further development, the location system should be enhanced to provide more accurate guidance for residents.

- Data entered is currently stored locally in the browser. Future implementations would include expanding Recollective into an app with data stored on a cloud server for better experience.

- Due to the fact that Recollective is currently operating in a web browser, it may be inconvenient to carry around during daily activities. Implementing hardware such as a smartwatch to detect stress levels and blood pressure, as well as Meta Ray-Ban glasses for a more portable form of computer vision, could further increase accessibility in the lives of people with dementia.
